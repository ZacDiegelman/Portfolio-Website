EGR 150 · Summer 2024 · Individual Project

Secure Delivery System

Designed and built a microcontroller-based secure delivery prototype intended to reduce package theft and monitor temperature-sensitive deliveries. A SparkFun RedBoard controlled an ultrasonic distance sensor, servo-actuated lid, temperature sensor, LCD display, LED, switch, and potentiometer.

PlatformSparkFun RedBoard
ControlArduino / C++
SensingDistance + Temperature
ActuationServo Motor
Arduino Sensor Integration Servo Control Breadboarding LCD Interface Prototyping Testing

The finished prototype automatically opened when a user approached, illuminated the enclosure, and continuously monitored internal temperature. The project also required repeated mechanical iteration to create a lightweight linkage that the small servo could reliably operate.

DFT 170 · Fall 2024 · CAD / Engineering Graphics

Pull Back Toy Car Design Blueprint

Recreated and documented a pull-back toy car assembly in SolidWorks, including the chassis, gearbox housings, gears, shafts, wheels, engineering drawings, and exploded assembly. The project then expanded the original mechanism into a conceptual car-plane redesign with new wings, steering hardware, supports, bevel gearing, and a rubber-band-driven propeller system.

The final presentation documents the complete original assembly and the added car-plane concept, including new component drawings, material selections, wing and steering mechanisms, propeller-drive components, and future design recommendations.
