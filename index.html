About Me

Background · engineering · experience · perspective

My name is Zechariah (Zac) Diegelman, and I am a Mechanical Engineering senior at UNC Charlotte. My path into engineering was not traditional. After high school, I spent several years working in hands-on roles including cabinetry, landscaping, construction and home-service work, crew leadership, and farm work. Those experiences taught me how to work with tools, solve problems in real time, adapt when plans change, communicate with customers and teammates, and take responsibility for the quality of the finished result.

I eventually realized that I wanted a career that combined that practical experience with a much deeper technical challenge. Mechanical engineering stood out because it brings together the things I enjoy most: designing, building, analyzing, testing, troubleshooting, and improving how things work. Since returning to school, I have earned an Associate in Engineering from Central Piedmont Community College and continued into the B.S. Mechanical Engineering program at UNC Charlotte.

I especially enjoy engineering work where an idea becomes something physical. My coursework has given me experience with SolidWorks, nTop, MATLAB, Python, C++, Arduino, LabVIEW, data acquisition, instrumentation, additive manufacturing, prototyping, and mechanical analysis. Some of my favorite work has involved building mechatronic systems, collecting real experimental data, developing CAD models, iterating printed parts, and comparing calculated or simulated behavior with what actually happens in a physical system.

My background has also shaped the way I approach people and challenges. I grew up as a missionary kid and lived in Costa Rica and Honduras, experiences that gave me a broader perspective and helped me become fluent in Spanish. My faith remains central to who I am and influences the way I try to work—with integrity, persistence, humility, and respect for the people around me.

Outside of engineering, I am married and enjoy spending time with my family and our Mini Australian Shepherd. I am motivated by growth—personal, professional, educational, and spiritual—and I want to build a career where I can keep learning while contributing to real products, systems, and engineering solutions.
